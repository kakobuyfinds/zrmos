A quick answer

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a product list used to organize shopping research: product names, images, source links, prices and selected options. It helps you compare finds before deciding what to order.

This version displays 12 real CAFANS listings. It is not a complete inventory or an official SuperBuy catalog. Check whether the original product link and chosen item are supported by your intended shopping agent before paying.

From discovery to decision

How to use this SuperBuy spreadsheet

Start with comparable products, then record enough detail to make a decision without relying on the thumbnail or headline price.

01 / Compare

Keep the exact variant

Open the CAFANS listing and record the product reference, selected size, color, seller link and current price. A single listing can contain different options. Confirm that the price you compare applies to the option you actually want.

02 / Measure

Make sizing concrete

For clothing, compare chest width, body length and shoulder measurements with a garment you already wear. For footwear, distinguish foot length from insole length and check the listing’s size chart. Save units and measurement methods alongside the numbers.

03 / Verify

Review before shipping

If you use a shopping agent, confirm purchase eligibility first. When item-specific warehouse photos are available, check quantity, color, size labels and visible condition. Ask about missing details and current return terms before authorizing international shipment.

Know the whole number

Plan beyond the product price

Keep item cost, domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international freight and destination charges separate. Convert all amounts to the same currency before adding them.

Request a shipping quote using packed weight, dimensions and destination. A low item price does not tell you how much the finished parcel will cost.

This worksheet adds your own figures. It does not retrieve shipping rates, tax rules or exchange rates. Blank fields remain unknown.

Useful answers

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official SuperBuy website?

No. This is an independent product-discovery page. Its catalog links lead to CAFANS. SuperBuy accounts, eligibility decisions, payments and shipping are not managed here.

Where do the two main buttons go?

Both “SuperBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” open https://cafans.com. Individual product cards open their corresponding CAFANS detail pages. The buttons do not create an order or start a SuperBuy checkout.

Are these actual products or example listings?

The 12 titles, images and reference prices were imported from the CAFANS homepage on November 19, 2026. They are source listings rather than invented examples. This saved preview may differ from the current catalog.

Can I order every CAFANS product through SuperBuy?

Compatibility has not been confirmed for these items. Open the current listing, locate the original seller link where available, and confirm that your agent supports both that link and the chosen product before paying.

Do prices include delivery and import charges?

No. The displayed USD amounts are reference estimates copied from CAFANS. Verify the current item price and currency, then obtain separate quotes for delivery, fees and any applicable destination charges.

Are the images warehouse QC photos?

They are catalog images from the source listings. They are not presented as inspections of your own purchased item. Request item-specific evidence through your chosen service when available.

Can I download this preview as a spreadsheet?

Yes. With JavaScript enabled, “Export CSV” downloads the currently filtered results with product names, categories, reference USD prices, source links and the snapshot date. Add your selected sizes, notes and updated quotes in your spreadsheet app.

Why can’t I find an item here?

The search covers only this 12-item preview across four categories. Clear the filters or open CAFANS to explore its broader catalog, including bags, accessories, headwear and other categories.
